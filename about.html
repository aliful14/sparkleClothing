About Sparkle

We're passionate about elevating fashion brands through premium RMG accessories that make a lasting impression.

Our Story

Sparkle is an extension of a Bangladesh-based garment accessories firm with over 35 years of industry experience. We specialize in providing a complete range of accessories — including labels, hang tags, trims, and packaging — designed to meet international standards of quality and compliance.

Our long-standing expertise in the Ready-Made Garments (RMG) sector has positioned us as a reliable partner for brands that value consistency, timely delivery, and sustainable solutions. Each accessory is produced with precision and attention to detail, ensuring it supports both the functionality and presentation of finished garments.

By combining decades of manufacturing knowledge with modern practices, Sparkle continues to serve as a trusted supplier to fashion brands worldwide.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower clothing brands with a full spectrum of reliable, sustainable, and globally compliant garment accessories — from labels and tags to trims and packaging. We combine three decades of heritage in Bangladesh with a modern, client-focused presence worldwide to help brands bring their vision to life.
